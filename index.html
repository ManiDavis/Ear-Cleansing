What We Offer

Comprehensive ear health care delivered professionally and safely

Ear Health Assessment

Before any treatment, a thorough examination of both ears is carried out using an endoscope — giving a clear, magnified view of the ear canal and eardrum to ensure the most appropriate care is selected.

Gold Standard

Endoscopic Microsuction

A tiny camera is gently placed at the ear canal entrance, providing a clear view of any blockage. A fine suction device then removes the wax safely — no water involved, suitable for all ear types including those with perforations or previous ear surgery.

Irrigation

A gentle flow of warm water is directed into the ear canal to soften and flush out wax. This well-established technique is effective for most patients, particularly following a course of softening ear drops beforehand.

Aftercare Advice

Every appointment concludes with personalised guidance on maintaining healthy ears — helping you prevent future build-up and stay comfortable between visits.
