What We Offer

Comprehensive ear health care delivered professionally and safely

Ear Health Assessment

A thorough examination of both ears to identify blockages, irritation, or any concerns — giving you a clear picture of your ear health.

Most Popular

Microsuction

The gold standard in wax removal. A gentle, dry procedure using a fine suction device — safe, fast, and highly effective even for stubborn blockages.

Irrigation

A water-based method to gently flush away ear wax — a tried-and-tested technique suited to most ear types, delivered with care and precision.

Aftercare Advice

Personalised guidance on keeping your ears healthy after your appointment, so you stay comfortable and blockage-free for longer.
